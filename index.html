About

Evidence before spend.

Most businesses treat ads as their growth engine. They're not — they're life support. The moment you stop paying, you disappear. I've watched it happen: CPCs climb, a campaign stops converting, an ad account gets suspended, and there's nothing underneath to fall back on.

That's why I started Clickvertise. Not to be another agency that runs your ads and sends you a report, but to change how a business thinks about where its customers come from. We do SEO. We run Google Ads. We build websites that convert. But the real work is helping a business show up on Google without paying for it, sell through its own site, and treat paid ads as a choice — not a dependency.

Alongside the agency, I work as an SEO Executive at Twister Technologies in Beirut. Before that: SEO for real estate and e-commerce brands at 88medias, Steps Real Estate Qatar and Delta Group in Istanbul, and content work at RoboLabs. I hold a BBA from the Lebanese International University and I'm fluent in Arabic and English.
