01 — About

If it's not ranking, I'll find out why.

Most businesses treat ads as their growth engine. They're not — they're life support. The moment you stop paying, you disappear. I've watched it happen: CPCs climb, a campaign stops converting, an ad account gets suspended, and there's nothing underneath to fall back on. That's the mindset I bring to every account I touch — build organic growth that compounds, and treat paid media as a choice, not a dependency.

I currently work as an SEO Executive at Twister Technologies in Beirut, and contribute performance marketing and SEO work at Clickvertise. Before that: SEO for real estate and e-commerce brands at 88medias, Steps Real Estate Qatar and Delta Group in Istanbul, and content work at RoboLabs.
